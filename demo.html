Meeting check-in

Recorded at the meeting, and again at the signature.

Most systems record that a resident tapped a button. InStep records where the phone was when the check-in started, and where it was when the witness signed.

01

Location captured

The resident’s GPS position is recorded when the check-in starts.

02

Meeting details

Type, name, and address of the meeting, entered on the resident’s phone.

03

Witness signs

The secretary, a sponsor, or another attendee signs on screen. A second GPS reading is taken at that moment.

04

One record

Timestamps, both locations, and the signature are saved together, ready to print.

Why the second reading matters. It shows the phone was at the meeting when the witness signed, not that the resident checked in early and got a signature somewhere else.
Curfew

One screen for the whole house.

Residents sign out and in from their phone. Managers see who is home, out, or late without a headcount.

  • Destination logged on every sign-out
  • Curfew by phase, with late sign-ins flagged automatically
  • Excused absences for a night or a date range, with the reason on record
Online meetings

A topic summary in place of a signature.

Online meetings have no one to sign in person. Residents describe what the meeting covered instead, and that summary is what appears on the record.

  • Clearly marked as online on every report
  • Topic summary required before the check-in saves
  • Counts alongside in-person meetings in the same weekly total

Your data is protected

Recovery records are sensitive. We treat them that way.

InStep is built so only the right people can see a resident’s information.

Login-gated

Nothing is visible without signing in. No public page exposes a resident’s information.

Walled off per house

Each house and organization is isolated from every other, enforced by the database itself.

PINs are scrambled

Sign-in PINs are stored as one-way hashes, never as plain text.

Encrypted in transit

Everything travels over HTTPS, the same encryption your bank’s website uses.

Every change on the record

A running log of who changed or removed what, by name.

Your data is yours

Export your records any time, and have them deleted on request. No lock-in.
