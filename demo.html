Why it matters

Accountability residents can stand on.
Compliance you can prove.

InStep turns the daily work of running a recovery residence into clean, verifiable records — keeping residents accountable and your house audit-ready.

🤝
Resident accountability
Witness-signed meeting check-ins with a GPS location stamp, curfew sign-in/out, and signed screenings. Every resident’s recovery work — recorded, timestamped, signed.
🛡️

Recovery happens in structure. InStep gives your house the structure — and the receipts.

What InStep does
🤝
Meeting check-ins
Witness-signed after every meeting, with a GPS location stamp. Permanent timestamped records.
🏠
Curfew tracking
Residents sign in and out. See real-time status across every house at a glance.
💉
Screenings
Log urine and breathalyzer results with dual signatures. Print-ready PDF every time.
📄
Document signing
House rules, intake forms — residents sign on their phone, you countersign. You get one audit-ready PDF: your original plus a signature page — the same approach e-sign tools like DocuSign use.
🗨️
Incident & grievance logs
Document incidents and resident grievances, track them to resolution, and print a confidential log for audits.
📊
Compliance reports
Attendance, screening, incident, and grievance reports ready to print. Built around the NARR Standard.
Your data is protected

Private by design.

Recovery records are sensitive. InStep is built so only the right people can ever see them.

🔒
Login-gated
Nothing is visible without signing in. There is no public page that exposes a resident’s information.
🧱
Walled off per house & organization
Each house’s data is isolated from every other house and every other organization — enforced by the database itself, not just hidden in the app.
🔑
PINs are scrambled
Sign-in PINs are stored as one-way hashes, never as plain text.
🛡️
Encrypted in transit
Everything travels over HTTPS, the same encryption your bank’s website uses.
📝
Every change, on the record
A running record of who changed or removed what — so you can always answer an audit, or a question.
📂
Your data is yours
Export your records any time, and have them deleted on request. No lock-in.
