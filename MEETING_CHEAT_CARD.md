## PART 1 — The Walkthrough  ·  tap → say

**Open on your phone/laptop first (so it's warm):** `instepapp.com`

**0. The opener (landing page)**
> "This is InStep. It turns the daily work of running a house into signed, timestamped records —
> so you run your house instead of the paperwork. Let me show you."
Tap **Launch demo** (or open `instepapp.com/?demo=resident`).

**1. Resident view** — recovery-day counter, meeting check-in, curfew
> "Every resident's recovery work — meetings, curfew in/out — recorded, GPS-stamped, and
> witness-signed. No more paper sign-in sheets that walk off."

**2. Manager view** (`?demo=manager`) — live board, screening, incident, grievance
> "You see who's in and out at a glance. Screenings sign both ways. Incidents and grievances get
> logged, signed, and tracked to resolution — and it all prints audit-ready in one tap."

**3. Owner view** (`?demo=owner`) — multi-house dashboard, Activity log, reports
> "Across every house you see everything, and pull an audit-ready report in seconds. There's a
> tamper-evident log of who changed what, by name. Built around the NARR documentation standard."

**4. Close**
> "It's live today. Your house could be running on it this week."

*If anything hiccups: "it's a live product — I'll note that," and move on. Don't apologize twice.*

## PART 2 — The Funding / Money Talk  ·  so you don't freeze

**Ground truth (this makes you calm):** InStep costs almost nothing to run — roughly **$50–150/mo total**
infrastructure across *all* customers. So "funding the launch" isn't covering big costs; it's her house
subscribing + a little runway so you can go sign the next houses.

**Let her lead the money.** She offered to fund it — so ask, then listen:
> "What did you have in mind for backing the launch?"
Then respond to *her* number. Don't talk yourself down.

**Your clear numbers (have these ready):**
- Standard: **$99/month per house**, unlimited residents, everything included.
- Founding thank-you (optional, your call): **$49/month** locked in, because she's first.

**What "yes" unlocks — say it plainly:**
> "If you're in: I set your house up this week — residents, managers — you're live, and you become
> the reference that gets me the next houses."

**If you don't know a number in the moment, this is a great answer:**
> "Let me get your house set up and delivering value first, then we land on the right number together."

**Don't undersell.** Her being your first paying house *and* your reference is worth real money to the
business. A yes here is the whole thing.

## If you get a hard question
- **Rent/payments?** → "Not in-app yet — rent stays on your current method; in-app billing is on the
  roadmap." (True, and operators respect that you say it first.)
- **Something you don't know?** → "Great question — let me confirm and get right back to you." Never bluff.
- **Security/privacy?** → "Login-gated, each house walled off in the database itself, PINs hashed,
  every change on the record. Nothing's public."
